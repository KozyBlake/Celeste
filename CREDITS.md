# Credits

## Scarlet

Celeste is a rewrite of **Scarlet**, written by **Vinyarion** of the
**Sybyline Network** and released under the MIT License. The structure
of the audit rendering, the moderation flows and the event handling all
follow Scarlet's design, and a great many of Celeste's source files are
ports of a specific Java file rather than original work.

This is a rewrite of their work, not a replacement of their authorship.
The applicable Scarlet copyright and licence notices remain in place,
and the credits list Celeste shows under **Help** remains as it was.

Scarlet's logo is not included here and is not Celeste's. It belonged
to that project and was never ours to carry over.

## Maintainer

**KozyBlake** — <https://github.com/KozyBlake>

Celeste is developed largely with AI assistance. Features and fixes come
from KozyBlake and from community ideas, are implemented and reasoned

who maintains the project and is accountable for it. The AI is a tool,
not the maintainer.
